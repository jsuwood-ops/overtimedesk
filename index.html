You’re on a rooftop unit. The phone’s ringing in the truck.

Every HVAC owner knows the drill. You can’t answer, and urgent callers don’t wait.

  • Peak season swamps the phone. The first muggy July heat wave or January cold snap brings more calls than anyone can pick up.
  • Urgent callers don’t wait. No heat, no AC: they call the next company on the list.
  • Web forms sit until tonight. By the time someone replies, the homeowner has booked.
  • After-hours calls go to voicemail. Nobody owns the callback until morning.
  • Reviews and tune-ups slip. You meant to ask every customer. It’s been a long season.

Companies that tried to contact online leads within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify them as companies that waited longer. Harvard Business Review, 2011

Straight answers

Will my customers know they’re talking to AI?

Yes. Our AI receptionist says it’s an automated assistant at the start of the call, and it never claims to be a person. If a caller wants a human, or the call is an emergency, it hands off to your team.

Are calls recorded?

If recording is on, the greeting says so at the start of every call.

Is it legal to text people who called me?

Missed-call text-back is a reply to someone who just called you. The first text names your business, explains why you’re texting, and includes “Reply STOP to opt out.” We also post a short texting notice next to your phone number and in your voicemail greeting, and we register your business with the carriers (A2P 10DLC) before any texts go out.

What about texting my old customer list?

Marketing texts need the right prior consent, usually written consent. We check how each contact’s consent was collected before any campaign. Contacts without clear consent get email instead.

What happens when someone texts STOP or HELP?

STOP unsubscribes them right away, with one confirmation text and nothing after unless they text START. HELP gets them your contact info. Non-urgent texts are kept to reasonable hours.

Do you make cold calls or send cold texts for me?

No. No cold texts and no automated or AI cold calls. The AI only calls back people who asked to be called.

How long until it’s live?

14 days, or your setup fee back. What “live” means: calls forward from your existing number (no porting), and lead follow-up and review requests go out by email. On Growth and Pro, the AI receptionist is answering too. Texting is submitted for carrier approval and switches on as soon as carriers approve. That approval (typically 5 business days to 3 weeks) doesn’t count against the 14 days. The clock starts once we have everything from intake, and pauses while we’re waiting on you for access or info.

Do I need a new number or new software?

No new number. We forward calls from your existing number rather than porting it, and connect to your calendar and field-service software where they allow it.

What area do you serve?

Residential HVAC companies within about 75 miles of Louisville, including Lexington, Elizabethtown, Shelbyville, Bardstown and southern Indiana (New Albany, Jeffersonville, Clarksville).

Is there a contract? Who owns my data?

The first term is 3 months, then it’s month-to-month. Who owns your data? You do. Your customer data stays yours, and we’ll export it for you if you leave.
