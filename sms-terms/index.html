1. Program name and description

Program name: Overtime Desk Inquiry & Customer Care Texts

We send conversational, non-marketing text messages about inquiries you start and services you use with us:

  • Missed-call follow-up: if you call our business line at (502) 660-8430 and we can't answer, we may send one text to the number you called from about your call. We may send follow-up texts to continue that conversation (for example, to answer your question or schedule a callback).

2. How you opt in

You opt in by calling our business line:

We do not send cold texts and do not buy or rent phone lists.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies with your inquiry or account activity. For a missed call, we usually send one text and at most one follow-up unless you reply.

4. Costs

Msg & data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's standard messaging and data rates apply. We don't charge for these texts.

5. Opting out (STOP)

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You can also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT. We'll send one confirmation text, and after that you won't get any more texts from this program unless you opt back in. To opt back in, reply START.

6. Help (HELP)

Reply HELP for help, or contact us at hello@overtimedesk.com or (502) 660-8430.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery depends on your carrier and device.

8. Quiet hours

A missed-call text goes out right after the missed call. Any other follow-up texts are sent only between 10 a.m. and 8 p.m. recipient-local time, Monday through Saturday.
